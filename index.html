For the site owner

Owner Publish Kit — how you host this & make money

Boss, this is your ready-to-publish draft. It is a single fast static site (one HTML file + images) — no database, no plugins, nothing to update or get hacked. Here is the exact go-live path:

Host it free & connect a domain

  1. Create a free account on Cloudflare Pages, Netlify or Vercel.
  2. Upload this folder (index.html + assets/ + the root files robots.txt, llms.txt, sitemap.xml and .well-known/agent-card.json that ship with this draft) — drag & drop on Netlify Drop is fastest. Those root files are what fix the PageSpeed robots.txt, llms.txt and agent-manifest warnings.
  3. Buy a domain (e.g. nestnookhome.com — check availability first) on Cloudflare or Namecheap.
  4. In your host, click Add custom domain and follow the 2 DNS records it shows. HTTPS is automatic & free.
  5. Submit the site in Google Search Console → Sitemaps. Speed is already handled: optimised images, lazy loading, one file, no heavy plugins.

How it earns

  • Affiliate (first $): Amazon Associates + Walmart / Target affiliate links on every “Best…” guide — replace the pick names with your affiliate links.
  • Ads (scale $): Apply for Google AdSense after ~30 days of traffic, then Ezoic / Mediavine Journey as traffic grows.
  • Email list: The checklist above is your asset — promote your best guides to it weekly.
  • Pinterest + YouTube Shorts: Repurpose each guide's visual — US home content travels very well there.

SEO already built in

  • Keyword-led titles, meta description, Open Graph + Blog schema
  • One clear search intent per post, internal links via categories & related posts
  • Fast, mobile-first, semantic headings
