Privacy Policy

Short version: NestNook collects very little about you. We do not run visitor analytics, we do not ask you to create an account, and we do not sell personal information. This page explains the details, including how advertising cookies work if ads are shown.

Last updated: 10 November 2026 · Applies to https://nestnook.site/

Who we are

NestNook (“we”, “us”) is a blog about renter-friendly smart homes, published by DigiTera. You can reach us any time through our Contact page.

What we collect — and what we don’t

  • Reading the site: you can read all 108 guides without giving us your name, email address or any account details. We do not ask you to sign up to read.
  • No analytics right now: we do not currently use Google Analytics or any other visitor-analytics tool on this site. If we add one later, we will update this page first.
  • Messages you send us: if you contact us through WhatsApp from our Contact page, WhatsApp handles that message under its own privacy policy. We only use what you send to reply to you, and we do not share it with anyone else.
  • Newsletter: we do not currently collect email addresses. Our newsletter is not live yet; when it launches, this page will say exactly what we collect and how to unsubscribe.

Cookies and advertising

NestNook itself sets no cookies of its own. Advertising is the one exception to know about:

  • When ads are shown on this site, third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You may also opt out of a third-party vendor’s use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices.
  • You can also control cookies in your browser settings, including blocking third-party cookies entirely. The site will still work if you do.

Affiliate links

Some guides link to products on Amazon, Walmart, Target and other retailers. If you buy through those links, NestNook may earn a commission — at no extra cost to you. Affiliate links do not change our recommendations, and they do not give us access to your purchase or payment details.

Links to other websites

Our guides link to manufacturers, retailers and sources such as Ring, Blink, Aqara and SwitchBot. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites; their own privacy policies apply once you leave nestnook.site.

Children’s privacy

NestNook is written for adults renting or furnishing a home. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please tell us through the Contact page and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we collect or use data — for example, by adding analytics, a newsletter provider or advertising — we will update this page and change the “Last updated” date above. The version on this page is always the current one.

Questions about this policy? Contact NestNook.
